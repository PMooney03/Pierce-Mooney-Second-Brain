# CloudSync Pro — Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Product Overview

CloudSync Pro is a cloud file synchronization service for individuals and small teams. It keeps files in sync across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android. Plans include Free (5 GB), Pro ($9.99/month, 100 GB), and Team ($24.99/month per user, 1 TB shared pool).

## Getting Started

New users sign up at cloudsyncpro.example.com. After email verification, download the desktop app or mobile app and sign in. The setup wizard walks you through choosing folders to sync. Initial sync may take time depending on file size and connection speed.

## Billing and Subscriptions

Pro and Team plans bill monthly on the anniversary of signup. Invoices are emailed within 24 hours of charge. You can update payment methods under Account → Billing. Annual billing is not offered on Free or Pro; Team customers may request annual invoicing by contacting sales.

## Refund Policy

Pro subscriptions include a 30-day money-back guarantee from the first paid charge. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Team plans are non-refundable after the first billing cycle unless required by law. Free plans have no charges and no refunds.

## Cancellation

You may cancel Pro or Team anytime from Account → Subscription → Cancel. Access continues until the end of the current billing period. After cancellation, synced files remain in your account for 90 days on Pro and 180 days on Team before read-only archive; download exports before that date.

## Password and Account Security

Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) under Account → Security. Supported methods: authenticator app and SMS (Pro and Team). Passwords must be at least 12 characters. We never ask for your password by email. Report suspicious emails to security@cloudsyncpro.example.com.

## File Sharing

Share files or folders via link or named collaborators. Link shares can be view-only or edit; set expiration from 1 day to 1 year. Team plan includes admin controls to restrict external sharing. Free plan links expire after 7 days maximum.

## Sync Issues

If files are not syncing: check you are signed in, verify network connectivity, ensure you have not exceeded storage quota, and confirm the file is not open exclusively by another program. Use Help → Run Diagnostics to upload logs (no file contents) to support.

## Storage Limits

Free: 5 GB total. Pro: 100 GB. Team: 1 TB shared per workspace (not per user). Single file upload limit is 5 GB on all plans. Version history keeps the last 30 days on Pro and 90 days on Team.

## Integrations

CloudSync Pro integrates with Slack (notifications), Microsoft 365 (open in browser), and Zapier (automation) on Pro and Team. Free plan has no integrations. API access is Team-only; request keys from Account → Developer.

## Mobile Apps

iOS and Android apps support upload, download, offline folders (Pro and Team), and camera upload. Offline folders must be selected manually; they count toward storage quota.

## Support Hours and Contact

Email support: support@cloudsyncpro.example.com. Live chat: Monday–Friday 9 AM–6 PM US Eastern (Pro and Team). Free plan: email only, 48-hour response target. Team plan includes priority queue with 4-hour target on business days.

## SLA (Team Plan)

Team workspaces receive 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. Credits are applied as service days if uptime falls below threshold. SLA excludes scheduled maintenance announced 48 hours in advance. Submit claims within 30 days of incident.

## Data Privacy and GDPR

We process data in US and EU regions; Team admins choose region at workspace creation. Data Processing Agreement available on request. Users may export personal data from Account → Privacy → Export. Deletion requests are honored within 30 days.

## Troubleshooting Login

Clear app cache, reset password if needed, and confirm 2FA device time is correct. Corporate networks may block sync ports 443 and 8443—ask IT to allow *.cloudsyncpro.example.com.

## Beta Features

Beta features are opt-in under Account → Labs. They may change or be removed without notice. Do not use beta for production-critical workflows.
